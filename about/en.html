What I am working on

IRATXO is a focus app for Android: you choose how long you are going to work, and the village only grows if you actually sit down and do it.

Wishwell for Steam is a free Chrome extension that turns the Steam wishlist into a much sharper one: deals from other stores if you paste a free key, how long each game takes and whether it runs on Steam Deck, all in the same panel. And plenty more.

And there is an FPS that has been in the works for years and does not have a public name yet.
